# Team handbook

One professor and three students. Names, GitHub handles, assignments, and meeting times remain unassigned until agreed by the team.

| Proposed role | Responsibility | Review partner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Student A: adaptation lead | Learner state, planner, decision explanations | Student B |
| Student B: learning/evaluation lead | Curriculum, assessments, comparison design, analysis | Student C |
| Student C: platform lead | Interface, model adapter, events, reproducibility | Student A |
| Professor: research supervisor | Scope, methods, review guidance, interpretation | Whole team |

These are proposed work packages, not confirmed assignments. Rotate reviews and demonstrations. This document does not invite collaborators or assign authorship.

## Weekly meeting

Suggested agenda: 5 minutes progress, 10 minutes demo or research evidence, 10 minutes blockers and decisions, 5 minutes next tasks. Each student brings an artifact and a question. Never put participant identifiers or sensitive disclosures in public notes.

## Decision log template

Date / question / options / evidence / decision / owner / revisit trigger. Record unresolved questions. Professor review is needed for study design, participant data, outcome changes, and human-support procedures.

## Definition of done

A task has an artifact, acceptance criteria, relevant validation, peer review, and updated documentation. A demo is not evidence of learning benefit. Research findings require reproducible analysis and explicit limitations.

## Kickoff agenda

1. Choose learners and curriculum.
2. Agree on question and feasible comparison.
3. Assign owners and reviewers.
4. Decide budget, computing access, licensing, and ownership.
5. Determine institutional review and support requirements.
6. Set the first sprint and meeting schedule.
